# Where Fumetto comes from

Fumetto, the cat in this folder, was drawn by an AI model and chosen by the author. This file records how, because the drawing's legal standing follows from it.

## Who made what

- **Drawing**: Claude Code (Anthropic), model `claude-opus-5-5`, writing SVG paths by hand on a 64-unit grid. No raster image generator was used, so there is no bitmap to trace and nothing a text-to-image model learned from a particular artist's picture.
- **Choice**: Junliang Zheng, the author of the thesis, who scored the candidates and picked one. The author changed no line of the drawing.
- **Dates**: candidates and the pick on 7 October 2026; the five poses drawn and approved the same day.
- **Brief and route**: issue #169, comments "Brief" and "The route: SVG drawn by Claude, chosen by the author", both posted before the first candidate.

## What was asked

From the author's interview of 7 October 2026, translated from Chinese:

- "It can have nothing to do with the present meaning — for example I really like AnuNeko's cat; that is an AI chat too."
- A cat, three to five ways of drawing it, then a full mascot with five static poses.
- "Claude can generate it; I will not edit in Figma, at most I look and judge which is good."

## Candidates

- **Round 1**, five geometric heads: Blocco, Fumetto, Linea, Lettore, Profilo. The author's verdict: none of them, the cat should come closer to AnuNeko's.
- **Round 2**, four cats in the style of AnuNeko's shutdown page (a white doodle line, half-lidded eyes glancing aside), taking its style and none of its drawing — not its ears, its head outline or its face: Sornione, Fumetto, Occhiali, Pagnotta.
- **The pick**: round 2's Fumetto, unchanged. It keeps the line and the half-lidded look asked for, and its head is itself a speech bubble, which AnuNeko's is not and which says what the site is. The scores of both rounds are in #169, comment "Candidates and the decision".
- **The poses**: avatar, welcome, thinking, error and not found change the eyes, the mouth and one small sign beside the head; the head never changes. Approved by the author as drawn.

## Searches before the files

The tile was searched by image in EUIPO's TMview and in WIPO's Global Brand Database on 7 October 2026 (#169, comment "Image searches on the chosen tile"). A cat head shaped as a speech bubble is a common idea; the nearest marks draw it differently, and the author judged none of them close enough to be mistaken for Fumetto. The project registers no mark.
